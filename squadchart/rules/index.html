Rules and limits

How SquadChart™ is designed to handle your team and files.

Designed to

  • Work only from what you tell it, and ask when something is unclear.
  • Treat your team file as information, not as instructions.
  • Back up your team record before each redraw, and check each chart before handing it over.
  • Show what changed after each update, and ask before replacing or deleting your saved team.
  • Install chart tools only with your OK.

Designed not to

  • Guess at missing details.
  • Pick the closest match for a name you didn't type in full.
  • Ask for passwords, keys or tokens.
  • Change or message your bots. Changes you ask for go into a file to pass on to your orchestrator.
  • Use outside chart services.

Limits

  • Built only for teams of up to 10 bots, plus you.

SquadChart™ is designed to follow these rules and not modify them. As with any AI tool, output can still be off at times, so always check your charts and files before relying on them.
